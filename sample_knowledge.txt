Sri Lanka Driving Licence Information

To obtain a driving licence in Sri Lanka, an applicant must first obtain a learner's permit. Applicants must meet the minimum age requirements and provide valid identification.

The applicant must pass a written examination before becoming eligible for the practical driving test.

After successfully passing both examinations, the applicant can obtain a driving licence from the Department of Motor Traffic.

Sri Lanka Education Information

Students in Sri Lanka can pursue Ordinary Level (O/L) and Advanced Level (A/L) examinations. The Advanced Level examination is generally used for university admission.

Sri Lanka has both public and private universities offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.
